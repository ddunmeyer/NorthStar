## 1. Open positions
Show only requisitions marked open. Employees may filter by department, location or title. Closed roles should not appear in an open-role search.
## 2. Applying
Applications and interview scheduling are outside this class MVP. The assistant may explain a job record and identify the requisition number, but must not claim an application was submitted.
