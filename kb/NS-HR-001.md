## 1. Purpose and conduct
North Star Systems is a fictional Chicago-based technology company. Employees treat colleagues respectfully and report workplace concerns to HR. This handbook is demo content, not legal or employment advice.
## 2. Vacation and PTO
Submit planned vacation at least 10 business days before the start date. Manager approval is required. Up to 40 unused vacation hours may carry over into the next calendar year. Individual available balances come from the HR tool and already account for approved reservations; do not subtract them again. Pending requests are not approved.
## 3. Working arrangements
Eligible hybrid employees may work remotely up to 3 days each week, subject to manager approval and team coverage. Changes in schedule require discussion with the manager.
## 4. Privacy
Employees may view their own expense and leave records. A manager sees direct-report records only through an explicitly implemented, authorized manager workflow. The class MVP supports self-service views only.
## 5. Dress code
Business casual is the standard Monday through Thursday. Jeans in good condition are welcome on Fridays. Client meetings and customer site visits call for business professional dress on any day. Clothing with offensive wording or images, beachwear and athletic wear are not appropriate at work. Closed-toe shoes are required in the server room and equipment areas. On video calls with clients, dress as you would in the office. Employees who need a religious, cultural or medical accommodation should contact HR.
## 6. Sick leave
Full-time employees receive 40 hours of paid sick leave each calendar year, separate from vacation PTO. Sick leave may be used for your own illness or injury, medical appointments, or to care for an immediate family member. Tell your manager before your workday starts, or as soon as you are able. An absence of more than three consecutive workdays requires a note from a healthcare provider. Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out. This demo tracks vacation PTO balances only; individual sick leave balances are not available in the HR tool.
