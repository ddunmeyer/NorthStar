# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Preapproval
Business travel requires manager preapproval. Choose economy airfare and hotels up to USD 200 per night before taxes. Exceptions require written Finance approval.
## 2. Meals and receipts
Meal reimbursement is capped at USD 75 per day. Itemized receipts are required for every expense. Submit reports within 30 calendar days. Personal expenses are not reimbursable.
## 3. Status meanings
Pending means awaiting review; approved means approved but not yet paid; paid means payment completed. Totals must be grouped by currency and calculated from records, not inferred by the model.
