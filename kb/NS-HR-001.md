## 1. Purpose and conduct
North Star Systems is a fictional Chicago-based technology company. Employees treat colleagues respectfully and report workplace concerns to HR. This handbook is demo content, not legal or employment advice.
## 2. Vacation and PTO
Submit planned vacation at least 10 business days before the start date. Manager approval is required. Up to 40 unused vacation hours may carry over into the next calendar year. Individual available balances come from the HR tool and already account for approved reservations; do not subtract them again. Pending requests are not approved.
## 3. Working arrangements
Eligible hybrid employees may work remotely up to 3 days each week, subject to manager approval and team coverage. Changes in schedule require discussion with the manager.
## 4. Privacy
Employees may view their own expense and leave records. A manager sees direct-report records only through an explicitly implemented, authorized manager workflow. The class MVP supports self-service views only.
